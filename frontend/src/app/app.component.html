Weight logging

Measurements

Role in the expert system: Weight logs feed the WeeklyPattern which is inserted into Drools on every evaluation. The engine uses them to detect WeightStagnation (variance < 0.2 kg over the week) and WeightOscillation (>2 kg swing in 7 days). These trigger chain rules like CaloricAdaptationBlock and PlanChangeNeeded. Log your weight consistently (same time each morning) for accurate multi-day pattern detection.
